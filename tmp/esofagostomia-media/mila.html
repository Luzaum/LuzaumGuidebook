Product description

MILA Length-Adjustable Polyurethane Esophagostomy Feeding Tubes are designed to provide enteral nutritional support in veterinary patients requiring short- or long-term assisted feeding. Constructed from clear, non-irritating polyurethane, these tubes resist kinking while maintaining a thinner wall profile than comparable silicone tubes, allowing for a larger internal diameter without increasing the overall tube size. This design helps maximize flow while minimizing the amount of space occupied within the esophagus for improved patient comfort. The tube features a tapered open end with multiple side holes, a secure Y-port for feeding, medicating, and flushing, and can be adjusted to the desired length after placement by trimming the proximal end and reattaching the Y-port.

  • Clear, non-irritating polyurethane construction

  • Larger internal diameter than comparable silicone tubes of the same French size

  • Radiopaque stripe with centimeter markings
