# Ícones dos Mini-apps

Antibioticoterapia, Nutrição e Neurologia usam miniaturas das artes em `public/apps/`. O catálogo exibe os ícones em quadrados responsivos de até 132 px.

Fluidoterapia Vet usa a arte da gota fornecida pelo usuário, em `public/consulta-vet/reference-apps/thumbnails/fluidoterapia-vet-gota.webp`, com transparência.

Transfusão Sanguínea usa a arte da gota vermelha fornecida pelo usuário, em `public/consulta-vet/reference-apps/thumbnails/transfusao-sanguinea-gota.webp`, com transparência.

Escalas de Dor usa uma miniatura da arte gerada com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen em 12/11/2026, em `public/consulta-vet/reference-apps/escalas-dor.png`.

## Prompt da versão anterior — Fluidoterapia Vet

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new square veterinary miniapp icon. The attached APPLE Score icon is a style reference. Match its full-bleed uniform deep midnight navy background (approximately #030924), bold saturated glossy 3D collectible character sculpture, rounded volumes, expressive oversized eyes, dramatic studio rim lighting and bright polished highlights. Center the compact silhouette, filling about 85% of the square with safe margins. Make the animal, bag, and continuous tubing readable at thumbnail size. No words, letters, labels, watermark, outer border, or rounded mask. A friendly golden-brown floppy-eared dog with its forepaw clearly visible, receiving IV fluid. A large transparent cyan-tinted IV bag of clear saline sits suspended beside and above its shoulder, with a simple bright blue droplet emblem. A single visible translucent blue tube starts at the bottom of the bag and follows a clean arc to a small blue catheter hub taped to the top of the dog's front leg, just above the paw. Make the actual tube-to-hub connection unambiguous. No exposed needle. Golden orange against aqua cyan and midnight navy.

## Prompt da versão anterior — Transfusão Sanguínea

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new square veterinary miniapp icon. The attached APPLE Score icon is a style reference. ## Prompt final — Escalas de Dor

Create one square veterinary app icon for pain assessment. A beautifully sculpted orange cat face with attentive ears and subtly concerned expression next to a short curved gauge of green, yellow and orange segments representing a pain scale. Polished collectible 3D medical icon, readable at thumbnail size.
